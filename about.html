Our Story

From Freelance Hustle to a Full Growth Team

We started where many of our clients are today: helping online stores sell through social media, one reel and one ad at a time. The work grew because the approach worked — understand the customer deeply, create content they genuinely enjoy, and measure everything against sales.

Today, Ali Baba Marketing Pro brings that same discipline to brands in Pakistan, the US and beyond, with a special focus on e-commerce and DTC brands in fashion, beauty, jewelry and lifestyle. Our own AI fashion brand work keeps us honest: we test every format on real audiences before we recommend it to a client.

We are proudly based in Lahore, Pakistan and fully remote-friendly — which means world-class marketing without world-city agency retainers.

Mission & Values

What We Believe

Original, Always
